Parameter Guide
🌿

Nitrogen (N) — drives leaf & stem growth. Low N causes yellowing of older leaves. Get value from soil test report.

🌸

Phosphorus (P) — essential for root development and flowering. Deficiency shows as purple-tinted leaves.

💧

Potassium (K) — regulates water use and disease resistance. Low K causes brown, scorched leaf edges.

⚗️

pH — controls nutrient availability. Most crops prefer 6.0–7.5. Below 5.5 or above 8 severely limits growth.

🌡️

Temperature & Humidity — use your region's seasonal averages, not daily readings.

🌧️

Rainfall — annual average in mm. Enter 0 if your field is fully irrigated.
